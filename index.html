How it works

  1. We add a Crewleave number to your crew WhatsApp group.
  2. Someone writes “off Friday”. You reply yes or no in the same thread. No app. No login for staff.
  3. We file the request, the approval, and the leave year against that person. You can export the six-year record for payroll or your accountant.

Questions

Do you calculate holiday pay for us?

No. You or your payroll already do that. We store the figure and the leave days so you can show the record for six years. We are not your HR advisor and we don’t give employment-law advice.

Is this a legal requirement?

From 6 April 2026 UK employers must keep adequate annual-leave and holiday-pay records for six years. A spreadsheet can be enough. Crewleave is the version that gets filled in because it lives in WhatsApp.

What about sick days?

Holiday only in this version. Keep sickness off the group thread.
