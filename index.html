How it works

  1. 1You email us. We add a Crewleave number to the crew WhatsApp group that week.
  2. 2Someone writes “off Friday”. You reply yes or no in the same thread. No app. No login for staff.
  3. 3We file the request, the approval, and the leave year against that person. You export it for payroll or your accountant.

Questions

Do you calculate holiday pay?

No. You or payroll already do. We store the figure and the days. We are not your HR advisor.

Is a spreadsheet enough?

Yes. You can keep records in any format you reasonably think fit. Crewleave is for when that spreadsheet stays empty.

What about sick days?

Holiday only. Keep sickness off this thread.

What’s the six-year record?

UK employers now have to keep adequate annual-leave and holiday-pay records for six years. A spreadsheet is allowed. This is the version that gets filled in, because it lives in the group.

Is the first month free?

Yes. Use it with a live crew. Then £49 a month. Cancel anytime — including in that first month.
